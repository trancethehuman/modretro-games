name: toronto-worldbuilding
description: Research and design geographically coherent Toronto districts, streets, landmarks, and transit for Toronto Dispatch.
---

Read `DESIGN.md` and `docs/GEOGRAPHY.md`. Consult the source registry (`content/sources.json`) before adding geographic content.

Use official City of Toronto data for street topology and neighbourhood references, TTC maps/route pages for transit, and official landmark sources for locations. Record URL, review date, licence/attribution status, and the fact supported. An opened portal page alone does not verify downloaded geometry or every proposed location.

Preserve relative orientation, intersections, landmark positions, and neighbourhood character while compressing distance for cartridge-scale play. Distinguish geographic facts, provisional game locations, and invented depots. Treat informal neighbourhood names separately from administrative boundaries.

Transit route identity and a normal corridor are different from a live detour. Propose an explicit map era before adopting routings. Never describe a historical or simplified map as current navigation guidance.

Keep artwork original; do not trace proprietary maps or copy logos. Record licences before importing geometry, map imagery, or data. Update the source registry and notices when external content is included. Validate mission endpoints and reachability once a collision map exists.
