City of Toronto geographic information
========================================================================

Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence – Toronto.

Official licence, version 1.0:

Information provider: City of Toronto.
Source datasets, reviewed 2026-10-02:
Toronto Centreline:
https://open.toronto.ca/dataset/toronto-centreline-tcl/

https://open.toronto.ca/dataset/former-municipality-boundaries/

full official boundary polygon, map tiles and official imagery are not
